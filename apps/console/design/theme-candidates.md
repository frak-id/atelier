## Recommendation
**Graphite & Alabaster** is the optimal choice for Atelier's dual audience. While developers often lean toward the stark contrast of *Glacial Onyx* or the cyber-aesthetic of *Midnight Cobalt*, the warm neutrals of *Graphite & Alabaster* create an environment that feels less like a raw hacking tool and more like professional enterprise software (resembling Linear or Stripe). The subdued greys prevent eye fatigue during dense, long-running agent sessions for Builders, while the editorial typography and subtle motion convey the polish, safety, and trustworthiness Operators and executives demand when overseeing autonomous code execution in their infrastructure.
